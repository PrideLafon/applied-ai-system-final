# Activity Listening Guide

## Studying
- Keywords: study, studying, homework, exam, revision, reading, library, concentrate, concentration, quiet
- Typical energy: 0.20-0.45
- Typical danceability: 0.25-0.60
- Matching genres: lofi, classical, ambient
- Matching moods: focused, chill
- Tip: Steady tempo and few surprises keep working memory free; avoid high-energy tracks.

## Coding and Deep Work
- Keywords: coding, programming, debugging, deep work, writing, project, developer, focus
- Typical energy: 0.30-0.55
- Typical danceability: 0.45-0.70
- Matching genres: lofi, synthwave, ambient
- Matching moods: focused, chill
- Tip: Mid-tempo, loop-based music keeps rhythm in the background while you stay in flow.

## Workout
- Keywords: workout, gym, exercise, running, lifting, cardio, training, pump, fitness
- Typical energy: 0.80-0.98
- Typical danceability: 0.60-0.90
- Matching genres: pop, hip hop, rock, synthwave
- Matching moods: intense, happy
- Tip: Tempo around 120-160 BPM matches running and lifting cadence.

## Party
- Keywords: party, friends, dance, dancing, club, celebration, birthday, hangout, gathering
- Typical energy: 0.75-0.95
- Typical danceability: 0.80-0.95
- Matching genres: pop, hip hop, indie pop
- Matching moods: happy, intense
- Tip: High danceability and positive valence keep a crowd moving.

## Sleep and Winding Down
- Keywords: sleep, bedtime, night, wind, unwind, insomnia, nap, calm, drift
- Typical energy: 0.10-0.30
- Typical danceability: 0.20-0.45
- Matching genres: ambient, classical, jazz
- Matching moods: relaxed, chill
- Tip: Slow tempo under 70 BPM and high acousticness help the body slow down.

## Road Trip and Commute
- Keywords: road, trip, drive, driving, commute, highway, travel, journey, car
- Typical energy: 0.60-0.85
- Typical danceability: 0.50-0.75
- Matching genres: rock, indie pop, synthwave
- Matching moods: happy, moody
- Tip: Anthemic, mid-to-fast tracks help with alertness on long drives.

## Rainy Day and Reflection
- Keywords: rain, rainy, sad, melancholy, reflect, reflection, lonely, nostalgic, gloomy, cry
- Typical energy: 0.25-0.45
- Typical danceability: 0.40-0.60
- Matching genres: jazz, lofi, ambient
- Matching moods: moody, relaxed
- Tip: Low valence with warm acoustic textures lets the mood be felt rather than fought.

## Cooking and Weekend Mornings
- Keywords: cooking, kitchen, brunch, weekend, morning, coffee, breakfast, cleaning
- Typical energy: 0.40-0.65
- Typical danceability: 0.55-0.75
- Matching genres: jazz, indie pop, lofi
- Matching moods: happy, relaxed, chill
- Tip: Warm, groovy and light music makes chores feel shorter.
