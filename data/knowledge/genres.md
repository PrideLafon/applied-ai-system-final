# Genre Guide

## Lofi
- Keywords: lofi, lo-fi, beats, mellow, tape, instrumental
- Typical energy: 0.30-0.50
- Typical danceability: 0.55-0.65
- Matching moods: chill, focused, moody
- Tip: Dusty drums and warm samples; designed to sit in the background.

## Pop
- Keywords: pop, catchy, radio, hooks, chart, mainstream, singalong
- Typical energy: 0.75-0.95
- Typical danceability: 0.75-0.90
- Matching moods: happy, intense
- Tip: Hook-driven, polished production with strong danceability.

## Rock
- Keywords: rock, guitar, drums, band, riff, loud, garage, amp
- Typical energy: 0.70-0.95
- Typical danceability: 0.50-0.65
- Matching moods: intense, happy, moody
- Tip: Guitar-driven with high energy but only moderate danceability.

## Ambient
- Keywords: ambient, atmospheric, drone, soundscape, spacious, floating, meditation
- Typical energy: 0.15-0.30
- Typical danceability: 0.30-0.45
- Matching moods: chill, relaxed
- Tip: Textural and slow with very high acousticness; no strong beat.

## Jazz
- Keywords: jazz, saxophone, piano, swing, smooth, cafe, improvisation, trumpet
- Typical energy: 0.30-0.50
- Typical danceability: 0.45-0.60
- Matching moods: relaxed, moody, happy
- Tip: Warm acoustic instruments, relaxed tempo and expressive harmony.

## Synthwave
- Keywords: synthwave, retro, neon, synth, eighties, outrun, nightdrive, electronic
- Typical energy: 0.70-0.90
- Typical danceability: 0.70-0.80
- Matching moods: moody, intense
- Tip: Analog synth arpeggios and driving beats; great for night drives.

## Indie Pop
- Keywords: indie, indie pop, bright, dreamy, alternative
- Typical energy: 0.55-0.80
- Typical danceability: 0.65-0.85
- Matching moods: happy, chill
- Tip: Bright, lightly produced pop with an organic feel.

## Hip Hop
- Keywords: hip hop, rap, bars, cypher, flow, 808
- Typical energy: 0.75-0.85
- Typical danceability: 0.85-0.90
- Matching moods: intense, happy
- Tip: Rhythm-forward with the highest danceability of the catalog.

## Classical
- Keywords: classical, orchestra, sonata, symphony, composer
- Typical energy: 0.15-0.30
- Typical danceability: 0.25-0.35
- Matching moods: focused, relaxed
- Tip: Instrumental and dynamic; ideal for concentration and winding down.
