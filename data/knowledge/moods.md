# Mood Guide

## Happy
- Keywords: happy, joyful, cheerful, bright, positive, smile, sunny
- Typical energy: 0.60-0.90
- Typical danceability: 0.65-0.90
- Matching genres: pop, indie pop, hip hop
- Tip: High valence is the best predictor of a happy-sounding track.

## Chill
- Keywords: chill, laid back, easy, casual, lounge, mellow, hang
- Typical energy: 0.25-0.55
- Typical danceability: 0.40-0.65
- Matching genres: lofi, ambient, indie pop
- Tip: Low-to-mid energy with a gentle groove.

## Intense
- Keywords: intense, aggressive, angry, powerful, fierce, hard, adrenaline, hype
- Typical energy: 0.80-0.98
- Typical danceability: 0.55-0.90
- Matching genres: rock, hip hop, synthwave, pop
- Tip: Very high energy and fast tempo; valence can be low or high.

## Relaxed
- Keywords: relaxed, relax, relaxing, peaceful, serene, soothing, gentle, soft
- Typical energy: 0.20-0.40
- Typical danceability: 0.30-0.55
- Matching genres: jazz, ambient, classical
- Tip: Slow tempo with acoustic textures.

## Moody
- Keywords: moody, dark, brooding, atmospheric, melancholic, introspective, midnight
- Typical energy: 0.30-0.75
- Typical danceability: 0.45-0.75
- Matching genres: synthwave, jazz, rock, lofi
- Tip: Lower valence with a strong atmosphere.

## Focused
- Keywords: focused, focus, concentrate, productive, flow, attention, zone
- Typical energy: 0.20-0.45
- Typical danceability: 0.25-0.60
- Matching genres: lofi, classical, ambient
- Tip: Predictable structure and minimal lyrical distraction.
